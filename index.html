      What to do inside a moving Metro Train?

      1. Passenger Alarm (PEA) Pull the red emergency lever located near every carriage door to alert the train operator immediately.
      2. Two-Way Talkback (PECU) Press the talk-button on the Passenger Emergency Communication Unit to speak straight to the Train Operator.
      3. Station Controller / CISF Security personnel in blue uniforms and CISF jawans are stationed at every platform and AFC gate.
